# World Model Evaluation Product Skill

## Workflow

1. Separate job requirements into industry research, user/workflow discovery, dataset definition, evaluation design, iteration support, and safety/data governance.
2. Identify the end user and real task before proposing metrics. Distinguish creator-facing video generation from interactive environment/world-model tasks.
3. Convert qualitative goals into observable conditions, step sequences, expected state changes, failure tags, and annotation guidance.
4. Define data provenance and governance fields: source/authorization, sensitivity, access, retention, deletion, model version, prompt, settings, and reviewer confidence.
5. Use a small double-annotated pilot to detect ambiguous definitions before scaling. Report sample size, slices, disagreement, and known limitations with aggregate scores.
6. Make prototypes visibly distinguish illustrative UI/motion from real model outputs. Never fabricate model names, benchmarks, training results, user studies, or metrics.
7. Keep official product status distinct from research publications; verify fast-changing capabilities against primary sources and date the research note.

## Evaluation axes

**Video generation:** instruction adherence, identity/object continuity, temporal stability, action sequence, camera control, audiovisual alignment, editability, safety, and provenance.

**Interactive world models:** action response, state persistence, causal consequences, spatial consistency across viewpoints, controllability, multi-step stability, and safety.

## Output checklist

- Role and user workflow are explicit.
- Each case has inputs, expected observations, failure labels, and review rules.
- Scores have anchored definitions and no unsupported quality claims.
- Examples are labeled synthetic when invented for UI demonstration.
- Product capabilities and dates cite official sources.
- Personal resumes and private contact details are kept out of public repositories.
